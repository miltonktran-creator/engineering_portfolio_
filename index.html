FRC 9470 2025 Robot

CAD / DESIGN / ASSEMBLY
Mechanical Design Lead • Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
OVERVIEW

I developed the overall robot architecture, using WCP Swerve X2T modules to maximize internal space for electronics and mechanisms. I helped conceptualize a significantly lighter end effector, using bottom rollers and a single motor to reduce its weight from nearly 7 pounds on our previous robot to just over 2 pounds, dramatically improving elevator speed. I also introduced a gravity-fed funnel with 3D-printed star wheels for automatic coral alignment and smooth feeding into the end effector. Its larger intake area and steeper angle enabled faster, more reliable game-piece collection than our previous powered funnel. These improvements resulted in a lighter, faster, and more optimized robot that ranked 16th, joined Alliance 6, and advanced to the third round of playoffs. Despite our overall performance, this remains my favorite robot I helped build on Team 9470.

FRC 9470 2026 Robots

CAD / DESIGN / ASSEMBLY
Mechanical Design Lead • Jan 2026 – Present
OVERVIEW

For our first robot, I developed the architecture around WCP Swerve X2T modules to maximize internal space for electronics and mechanisms. I created a 25-inch-wide intake capable of collecting four fuel balls simultaneously and integrated a roller-powered hopper floor to improve fuel transfer speed. I also implemented a three-lane shooter to maximize fuel throughput while maintaining a simple design with fewer moving parts than turret-based systems.

For our second robot, I rebuilt the robot from scratch using the SDS MK5n swerve base, improving intake durability and hopper rigidity. I redesigned the hopper floor with compliant wheels and lightweight carbon fiber rollers to reduce weight and improve fuel flow, while making the floor rotatable for easier access to electrical systems. I also rebuilt the shooter into a full-width drum to maximize fuel output. Competition experience revealed further areas for improvement, including intake durability, hopper clearance beneath the trench, and shooter hood vibration that reduced aiming accuracy.

PRJ / 003

Custom CNC Router

DESIGN / MANUFACTURE / ASSEMBLE
Mechanical Design Lead • Jan 2025 – Mar 2025
OVERVIEW

The CNC Router was my third major project and the first where I integrated mechanical design, electronics, and software into a single project. The goal was to create a computer-controlled cutting device capable of machining wood and plastic with decent accuracy, allowing me to level up the parts I could make for future projects as well as use it as an engraver for gifts and stationery. All the custom parts were 3D printed from Carbon Fiber PETG to maxamize rigidity and precision. The structure was constructed from 2020 aluminum extrusion, and the motion system was driven by NEMA 17 stepper motors with GT2 timing belts. I designed and built a custom spindle mount to hold a DeWalt hand router as the spindle, and I used an Arduino Uno with GRBL firmware to control the machine. The project was a success, and I was able to cut various materials with good precision, which opened up new possibilities for my future projects.

Onshape CNC machining Arduino Uno

PRJ / 004

2 AXIS GIMBAL

CAD / BUILD / PROGRAM
Design Engineer • Jul 2026 – Sep 2026
OVERVIEW

Designed and developed a two-axis robotic mechanism capable of positioning an end effector in a 2D plane using two motors driving interconnected arms in a parallelogram configuration. Dedicated two weeks to designing, testing, and performing Finite Element Analysis (FEA) on the cycloidal drives used to power the mechanism, evaluating their structural performance and refining the designs. Derived the inverse kinematics equations by hand to calculate the required motor angles for a given (x,y) end-effector coordinate, establishing the mathematical foundation for precise planar positioning.
